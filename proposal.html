11 — THE COLD CALL PLAYBOOK

The 45-Second Cold Call Architecture.

Cold calling enterprise decision-makers in Nigeria fails when treated as a sales pitch. It succeeds when executed as an operational risk diagnostic.

01 Pattern Interrupt (5s) Disarm & ask permission: "Good morning [Name], I'm [Rep] with New Era Transport (NETS). I know you're running operations at [Company], so I'll be brief—do you have 30 seconds?"
02 Operational Hook (10s) Expose the exact bottleneck: Name the cluster's specific pain point—shift line stoppage, Shell/NNPC audit compliance failure, 3-hour commute burnout, or late-night staff vulnerability.
03 SLA Contrast (15s) Position the infrastructure difference: Introduce NETS' 15-minute replacement standby SLA, real-time IVMS tracking, and fuel-hedged monthly billing.
04 Frictionless CTA (10s) Never sell the contract on the call: "Could we schedule a brief 10-minute discovery call this Thursday to share a complimentary 48-hour route efficiency and cost benchmark?"
•

Golden Rule of Enterprise Outreach: The only goal of a cold call is securing the Route Benchmark Audit or Technical Pre-Qualification Meeting—not closing the deal on the phone.

14 — OBJECTION HANDLING & CONVERSION

Mastering Objections & The Trojan Horse Strategy.

The 5 Toughest Objections

"We already have a transport provider."

"Understood. Most tier-1 firms do. We don't ask you to switch today. We offer a free 48-hour route benchmark so when your contract comes up for annual tender, you have an exact price and SLA benchmark to hold your current vendor accountable."

"Just send an email to info@..."

"Happy to send it over. But so I don't send a 15-page generic brochure: are you most focused on cutting route fuel costs, fixing morning lateness, or meeting HSE compliance?"

"We give our staff transport allowances instead."

"Cash allowances lose value with fuel spikes while staff still sit in 3 hours of traffic. A corporate shuttle guarantees arrival time, reduces salary pressure, and turns commute hours into productive rest."

"Your price is higher than regular bus rentals."

"A breakdown on the expressway costs ₦3M+ in lost factory output per hour. Our rate includes an immediate standby replacement bus, IVMS tracking, and fuel hedging. We price per productive employee arrival."

"Our contract ends in December, call us then."

"Corporate procurement and vendor registration take 6–8 weeks. Starting the paperwork and running a single-route pilot now ensures zero disruption when your current contract expires."

The Conversion Mechanism

High-value B2B contracts are won by lowering the initial risk barrier to zero.

TROJAN HORSE STEP 1

The Complimentary 48-Hour Route & Cost Benchmark

The prospect provides employee residential counts and shift timings. NETS returns an executive 2-page blueprint mapping optimal stop clusters, fuel expenditure savings, and arrival schedules.

TROJAN HORSE STEP 2

The Single-Route 30-Day Pilot

Rather than replacing their entire fleet, NETS takes over their single most problematic route (e.g. Mainland-to-Island or Agbara 6 AM shift) to prove 99%+ punctuality and 15-minute standby responsiveness before full fleet rollout.

Pipeline Metric: Every 10 completed Route Benchmarks generate 4 Pilot Routes, which convert into 2–3 multi-year enterprise contracts.
