02 — THE COMMERCIAL PROBLEM

Marketing activity doesn't automatically produce corporate contracts.

Corporate transportation is a high-consideration B2B purchase. A company weighing a vendor is asking one question, and it isn't the question most marketing answers.

NOT THIS

"Have I seen this company before?"

BUT THIS

"Can this company reliably solve our transportation problem?"

So the marketing system has to be designed around the buying journey, not around reach.

07 — THE VIDEO CONTENT ENGINE
