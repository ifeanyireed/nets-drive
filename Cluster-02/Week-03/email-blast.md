### Cover Story: Infrastructure & Energy Transit
**HEADLINE: Upstream Operators Intensify Journey Management Audits as Subcontractor Spot-Hires Trigger Marine Mobilization Stoppages**

*Photo: Heavy transport vehicles and support vessels at an oil and gas supply terminal in Rivers State. Source: Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC).*

Supermajors and marginal field operators across Port Harcourt and Onne have implemented unannounced gate audits targeting third-party personnel shuttles. During a recent audit cycle, over 40% of spot-hire buses engaged by engineering contractors were impounded at terminal gates due to missing In-Vehicle Monitoring System (IVMS) units, uncertified speed limiters, or non-functional 3-point seatbelts, stalling scheduled crew vessel departures and incurring major contractual penalties.

> **► WHAT IT MEANS FOR LOGISTICS & HSE MANAGERS:**
> Spot-hire crew transit is a severe project risk. When a charter bus is rejected at the Onne Gate or Eleme checkpoint, specialists miss crew boats, leaving offshore construction barges and drilling rigs idling at spread rates of $50,000 to $85,000 per day. Energy contractors must eliminate spot-hires and enforce Master Service Agreements with audited, IVMS-compliant corporate fleet operators.
