Upstream Route Alert

Federal Ministry of Works Accelerates Eleme-Onne Section of East-West Road as IOCs Tighten Journey Compliance Audits

The Federal Government and Rivers State authorities have intensified dual-carriageway reconstruction along the critical Eleme-Onne segment of the East-West road. While long-term port accessibility will improve, active roadbed excavation and bridge diversions have heightened transit congestion. Concurrently, major operators (Shell, Chevron, TotalEnergies) have tightened terminal checkpoint audits, turning back contractor buses lacking certified IVMS telematics or functional 3-point seatbelts.

► WHAT IT MEANS FOR ENERGY CONTRACTORS

Contracting informal roadside buses to save ₦150,000 per trip is an unacceptable corporate gamble. When an uninspected vehicle is rejected at the Onne security gate, the entire scheduled crew boat departs empty. Contractors face immediate contractual HSE penalties, potential de-registration from the NIPEX portal, and offshore platform standby demurrage averaging \$25,000 per day.

Energy Logistics & Compliance Round-Up

IOC Compliance

NUPRC & Major Operators Enforce Real-Time Telematics on All Contractor Crew Shuttles

Under updated Nigerian upstream journey management guidelines, third-party contractor personnel shuttles must transmit continuous satellite speed and GPS telemetry to operational control desks, disqualifying unmonitored spot charters.

Source: Upstream Energy Review / BusinessDay
Marine Mobilization

Onne Port Authority Implements 90-Second Digital Gate Pass Protocol

The Oil & Gas Free Zones Authority (OGFZA) has deployed electronic barcode gate clearances at Onne. Shuttles with pre-registered digital manifests clear security barriers in 90 seconds, bypassing manual paper checkpoints.

Source: OGFZA / The Guardian Nigeria
Safety Benchmarks

Level 3 Defensive Driving Accreditation Linked to 88% Drop in Corridor Incidents

An analysis of energy contractor logistics along the Port Harcourt - Warri corridor revealed that transport fleets staffed exclusively with Level 3 certified defensive chauffeurs maintained a 100% fatality-free record over 1.2 million kilometers.

Source: Petroleum Safety Council of Nigeria
