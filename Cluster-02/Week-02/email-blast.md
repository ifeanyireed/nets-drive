### Cover Story: Upstream Route & Regulatory Alert
**HEADLINE: Federal Ministry of Works Accelerates Eleme-Onne Section of East-West Road as IOCs Tighten Journey Compliance Audits**

*Photo: Heavy energy freight and contractor transit navigating construction diversions on the East-West road corridor near Eleme. Source: Rivers State Ministry of Works / Vanguard.*

The Federal Government and Rivers State authorities have intensified dual-carriageway reconstruction along the critical Eleme-Onne segment of the East-West road. While long-term port accessibility will improve, active roadbed excavation and bridge diversions have heightened transit congestion. Concurrently, major operators (Shell, Chevron, TotalEnergies) have tightened terminal checkpoint audits, turning back contractor buses lacking certified IVMS telematics or functional 3-point seatbelts.

> **► WHAT IT MEANS FOR ENERGY CONTRACTORS:**
> Contracting informal roadside buses to save ₦150,000 per trip is an unacceptable corporate gamble. When an uninspected vehicle is rejected at the Onne security gate, the entire scheduled crew boat departs empty.
