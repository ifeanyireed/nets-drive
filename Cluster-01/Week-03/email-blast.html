Executive Analysis

Manufacturing Benchmarks Reveal Cash Transit Allowances Generate 3x Higher Real Costs Than Managed Corporate Shuttles

An operational study across 140 continuous-process industrial plants in Ogun and Lagos States shows that facilities disbursing cash transport allowances experience an average of 14 delayed shift handovers per month, incurring over ₦30.5M in unrecoverable line downtime, idle captive gas turbine burn, and emergency night-shift overtime. In contrast, factories operating employer-managed dedicated shuttles with strict arrival SLAs eliminated 94% of shift handover lag.

► WHAT IT MEANS FOR PLANT OPERATORS

Cash allowances outsource your plant's production schedule to informal, volatile commercial transport. When machine operators compete for Danfos during morning rain or fuel pump price hikes, they arrive 35 minutes late. Replacing unorganized allowances with dedicated, corridor-mapped corporate shuttles saves an average of ₦26.5M monthly in direct downtime and overtime costs while guaranteeing 99.2% shift handover punctuality.
