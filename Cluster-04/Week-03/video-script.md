## Production Notes & Technical Specs

* **Total Word Count**: 620 spoken words (~4:15 runtime at 140 WPM).
* **Audio / SFX Direction**:
  - Night urban soundscape: Distant nocturnal traffic, wind, sharp click of heavy commercial door locks.
  - Hard acoustic contrast between vulnerable, eerie street silence and warm, safe, music-filtered shuttle cabin.
  - Rewind transition at 2:30 must feature an analog tape reverse sound layered with sub-bass drop.
* **Casting & Wardrobe**:
  - *Host*: Empathetic, sharp professional attire (slate knit polo, navy trousers).
  - *Mrs. Ngozi*: 40s, experienced retail operations director, tailored burgundy blazer with company pin, lanyard.
  - *Amina*: 20s, smart, hardworking cashier in clean retail uniform and apron.
  - *Chauffeur*: Polished corporate uniform, high-vis ID lanyard, respectful and professional demeanor.

  3. Close-up of digital tablet showing live GPS vehicle breadcrumb trail on dark map.
  4. Cashier stepping off shuttle under streetlamp and waving safely as residential gate closes.
