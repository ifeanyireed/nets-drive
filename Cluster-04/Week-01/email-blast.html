Frontline Safety Report

Retail Safety Audit: 54% of Urban Late-Night Store Resignations Linked to Transit Hazards & Street Harassment

An operational study across 140 supermarkets, quick-service food chains, and retail outlets in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt revealed that over 54% of departing female frontline workers cited midnight transit fear as their primary reason for leaving. Workers face acute robbery risks, commercial fare gouging, and severe transit deficits.

► WHAT IT MEANS FOR RETAIL & OPERATIONS DIRECTORS

Duty of care does not end when store shutters are pulled down. Leaving closing staff to navigate dangerous midnight public transit drives frontline turnover to over 45%, costs ₦250,000 per cashier replacement, and forces store managers to close registers early. Gated, employer-managed night dispersal shuttles eliminate turnover and secure late-evening sales.
