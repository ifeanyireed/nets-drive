## Production Notes & Technical Specs

* **Total Word Count**: 620 words (~4:15 runtime at 140 WPM).
* **Audio / SFX Direction**:
  - High emotional contrast between the eerie, threatening night-time junction ambience (echoing wind, distant traffic, harsh shouting) and the secure, bright environment of the NETS shuttle.
* **Casting & Wardrobe**:
  - *Host*: Sharp, empathetic retail corporate attire (charcoal dark jacket, black polo).
  - *Blessing*: Young, vulnerable, wearing a branded apron/uniform over civilian clothes, clutching bag.
  - *Emeka*: Seasoned retail operations executive, focused on margins and frontline staffing.
* **B-Roll Capture Checklist**:
  1. Low-angle tracking shot of retail security shutters rolling down at 10:30 PM.
  2. Shot of GPS dispatch screen showing live night vehicle locations with panic-button readiness.
  3. Close-up of female staff texting family from inside the brightly lit shuttle cabin.
  4. Busy morning retail queue with all tills fully manned and operating smoothly.
* **60-Second Social Cutdown Concept (LinkedIn Video / Ad)**:
  - *0:00–0:08*: Host cold open: "Why 10:30 PM retail staff face severe security hazards."
  - *0:08–0:25*: Quick cut: Dark junction terror → Empty tills the next morning.
  - *0:25–0:45*: Math breakdown: 45% staff turnover = ₦21M annual drain.
  - *0:45–1:00*: NETS night dispersal shuttle in staff bay → CTA to book a night dispersal route audit.
